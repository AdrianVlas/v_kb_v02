# MA-01 Candidate Revision r0002

## Status

CANDIDATE — CONTROLLED CONTENT REFINEMENT

## Purpose

Define the candidate role of MA-01 as a system-level baseline container for later, evidence-backed engineering context.

## Candidate scope

MA-01 may describe:

- the system context required to interpret downstream artifacts;
- the boundary between established source material and unresolved system assumptions;
- applicability conditions for a baseline;
- references to exact revisions of supporting artifacts, when those revisions are established.

## Authority boundary

MA-01 does not establish system authority by itself. Its authority remains unresolved while Q-ANA01 is open. No baseline is treated as normative, approved, fixed, or final.

## Candidate content rules

- distinguish source evidence, derived knowledge, and unresolved assumptions;
- preserve exact object and revision identifiers;
- avoid speculative relations;
- record applicability limits explicitly;
- keep implementation, verification, and acceptance results outside this object.

## Open questions

- Q-ANA01 — system-level authority and baseline applicability;
- Q-AUTHORITY-001 — authority assignment;
- Q-SOURCE-REVISION-001 — source revision basis.

## Exclusions

Production engineering content, final baseline designation, verification results, acceptance records, and implementation results are excluded.
