# MA-02 Candidate Revision r0002

## Status

CANDIDATE — CONTROLLED CONTENT REFINEMENT

## Purpose

Define the candidate governance record for decisions, constraints, unresolved questions, and controlled changes affecting the knowledge-base system.

## Candidate scope

MA-02 may record:

- governance decisions and their decision status;
- scope changes and exclusions;
- open questions and their impact;
- review findings and disposition;
- controlled changes to candidate artifacts.

## Authority boundary

MA-02 records governance information but does not invent governance authority. Decision authority, approvers, owners, and effective dates remain NOT ESTABLISHED unless supported by explicit evidence.

## Candidate decision rules

- distinguish proposed, candidate, provisional, and approved states;
- preserve the basis and affected objects for each decision;
- do not convert an unresolved question into an implicit decision;
- do not use MA-02 to approve production engineering content;
- keep acceptance and verification records separate from bootstrap governance.

## Open questions

- Q-AUTHORITY-001 — authority assignment;
- Q-ANA01 — system-level authority and baseline applicability;
- Q-BC07 — boundary and quality-status dependency.

## Exclusions

Invented owners, approvals, normative authority, production implementation decisions, verification results, and acceptance evidence are excluded.
