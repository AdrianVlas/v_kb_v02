# MA-03 Candidate Revision r0002

## Status

CANDIDATE — CONTROLLED CONTENT REFINEMENT

## Purpose

Define candidate operational rules for how roles and artifacts exchange, review, revise, and refer to controlled information.

## Candidate scope

MA-03 may describe:

- common interaction and handoff protocol;
- artifact intake, review, clarification, and revision steps;
- exact-revision reference handling;
- distinction between protocol rules and A→B or B→C contracts;
- treatment of unresolved questions and validation findings.

## Candidate protocol rules

- identify the producing and consuming role only when established;
- refer only to exact object revisions;
- preserve candidate or provisional status during unresolved review;
- record clarification requests instead of silently filling missing facts;
- keep operational protocol separate from contract obligations;
- do not infer normative relations from workflow convenience.

## Authority boundary

These rules are candidate operational guidance only. They do not establish role authority, ownership, approval, or acceptance.

## Open questions

- Q-AUTHORITY-001 — authority assignment;
- Q-SOURCE-REVISION-001 — source revision basis;
- Q-BC07 — boundary and quality-status dependency.

## Exclusions

Production implementation instructions, verified quality claims, acceptance criteria, and unconfirmed role ownership are excluded.
