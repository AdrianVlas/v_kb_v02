# MA-07 Candidate Revision r0002

## Status

CANDIDATE — CONTROLLED CONTENT REFINEMENT

## Purpose

Define candidate GUI requirements and specification content as a distinct input to later design work.

## Candidate scope

MA-07 may define:

- user-facing and system-facing GUI requirements;
- functional behavior and interaction expectations;
- display, navigation, state, and error-handling requirements;
- requirement provenance and exact revision references;
- unresolved requirement questions and constraints.

## Separation rules

- requirements are not GUI design;
- requirements are not implementation code;
- a requirement must retain its source or rationale when established;
- ambiguity must remain visible and trigger clarification;
- quality or usability claims remain provisional while Q-BC07 is open.

## Authority boundary

Requirement authority, approval, ownership, and completeness are NOT ESTABLISHED. This object is not an approved specification.

## Open questions

- Q-BC07 — boundary and quality-status dependency;
- Q-SOURCE-REVISION-001 — source revision basis;
- Q-AUTHORITY-001 — authority assignment.

## Exclusions

GUI design identity, implementation results, verification, acceptance, and untraceable requirements are excluded.
