# MA-08 Candidate Revision r0002

## Status

CANDIDATE — CONTROLLED CONTENT REFINEMENT

## Purpose

Define candidate GUI design content that interprets requirements into visual, interaction, and structural design decisions.

## Candidate scope

MA-08 may define:

- screen and component structure;
- navigation and interaction design;
- layout, visual states, and display behavior;
- design rationale and traceability to MA-07 revisions;
- explicit dependencies on external design references.

## Separation rules

- design is distinct from requirements;
- an external Penpot or other design reference is evidence/input, not automatically the MA-08 identity;
- design decisions must not be presented as implementation results;
- unresolved design choices remain candidate;
- no design relation is normative without confirmed basis.

## Authority boundary

Design ownership, approval, completeness, and external-reference authority are NOT ESTABLISHED.

## Open questions

- Q-SOURCE-REVISION-001 — source revision basis;
- Q-BC07 — boundary and quality-status dependency;
- Q-AUTHORITY-001 — authority assignment.

## Exclusions

Embedded implementation, verification, acceptance, and unconfirmed external design authority are excluded.
