# MA-05 Candidate Revision r0002

## Status

CANDIDATE — CONTROLLED CONTENT REFINEMENT

## Purpose

Define the candidate knowledge-base container that separates source evidence, derived knowledge, and controlled references used by downstream artifacts.

## Candidate scope

MA-05 may contain or reference:

- source-derived facts with provenance;
- normalized knowledge statements;
- unresolved interpretations;
- exact revision references;
- applicability and exclusion notes;
- links to knowledge packages without becoming their substitute.

## Separation rules

- source evidence is not silently converted into normative knowledge;
- derived knowledge must retain its basis;
- unresolved interpretation remains marked as unresolved;
- implementation content does not belong in the knowledge base;
- the knowledge base does not replace external source repositories.

## Authority boundary

MA-05 is a candidate knowledge container. It does not establish source authority, engineering truth, or approval status. Authority and source revision basis remain unresolved where the open questions apply.

## Open questions

- Q-SOURCE-REVISION-001 — source revision basis;
- Q-ANA01 — system-level authority and baseline applicability;
- Q-AUTHORITY-001 — authority assignment.

## Exclusions

Untraceable claims, speculative relations, production implementation content, verification results, and acceptance records are excluded.
