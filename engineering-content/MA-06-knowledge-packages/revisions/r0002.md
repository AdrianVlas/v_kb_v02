# MA-06 Candidate Revision r0002

## Status

CANDIDATE — CONTROLLED CONTENT REFINEMENT

## Purpose

Define candidate knowledge packages as bounded, reusable groupings of knowledge for a stated purpose, scope, and consumer.

## Candidate scope

MA-06 may define:

- package identity and intended purpose;
- membership rules and scope;
- source and revision basis;
- consumer constraints;
- package-level exclusions and unresolved issues;
- lifecycle from candidate package to later controlled revision.

## Membership rules

- package membership is explicit and candidate until confirmed;
- a package must not silently duplicate the complete MA-05 knowledge base;
- each member retains its own identity and exact revision;
- package scope does not create authority over its members;
- quality or usability claims remain provisional while Q-BC07 is open.

## Authority boundary

MA-06 does not establish package ownership, approval, or downstream acceptance. Membership, owner, and consumer authority remain NOT ESTABLISHED unless evidenced.

## Open questions

- Q-BC07 — boundary and quality-status dependency;
- Q-SOURCE-REVISION-001 — source revision basis;
- Q-AUTHORITY-001 — authority assignment.

## Exclusions

Unbounded membership, speculative relations, implementation results, verification evidence, and acceptance status are excluded.
