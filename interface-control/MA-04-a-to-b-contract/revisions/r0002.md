# MA-04 Candidate Revision r0002

## Status

CANDIDATE — CONTROLLED CONTENT REFINEMENT

## Purpose

Define the candidate A→B contract boundary between upstream knowledge/requirements work and downstream GUI-oriented specification work.

## Candidate scope

MA-04 may define:

- required inputs crossing the A→B boundary;
- expected semantic meaning and completeness conditions;
- obligations for preserving source and revision references;
- clarification handling when inputs are incomplete or ambiguous;
- exclusions that prevent design or implementation content from crossing this boundary.

## Boundary rule

MA-04 describes the transfer contract only. It does not define the internal workflow of either side and does not replace MA-03 operational protocol.

## Candidate contract rules

- each transferred item must identify its object and exact revision when established;
- unresolved or missing information must remain explicitly unresolved;
- assumptions must not be presented as requirements;
- downstream consumers may request clarification without silently rewriting the source;
- implementation decisions and visual design results are outside this contract.

## Authority boundary

Contract authority, producer identity, consumer identity, and approval conditions remain NOT ESTABLISHED.

## Open questions

- Q-BC07 — boundary and quality-status dependency;
- Q-AUTHORITY-001 — authority assignment;
- Q-SOURCE-REVISION-001 — source revision basis.

## Exclusions

Production requirements, GUI design decisions, implementation results, verification, and acceptance evidence are excluded.
