# MA-09 Candidate Revision r0002

## Status

CANDIDATE — CONTROLLED CONTENT REFINEMENT

## Purpose

Define the candidate B→C contract boundary between GUI design/specification work and implementation input/work.

## Candidate scope

MA-09 may define:

- the design and requirement inputs delivered to implementation;
- required traceability and exact revision references;
- implementation-input completeness conditions;
- clarification handling for unresolved design decisions;
- exclusions that prevent implementation results from entering the contract.

## Boundary rule

MA-09 defines the transfer contract only. It does not contain implementation source code, build results, test results, or acceptance evidence.

## Candidate contract rules

- every transferred item must identify its source object and exact revision when established;
- unresolved design decisions must be marked unresolved;
- implementation may request clarification rather than silently infer missing requirements;
- external references must retain their declared status and basis;
- quality claims remain provisional while Q-BC07 is open.

## Authority boundary

Contract authority, producer identity, consumer identity, approval, and acceptance conditions remain NOT ESTABLISHED.

## Open questions

- Q-BC07 — boundary and quality-status dependency;
- Q-AUTHORITY-001 — authority assignment;
- Q-SOURCE-REVISION-001 — source revision basis.

## Exclusions

Implementation code, build artifacts, verification results, acceptance records, and unconfirmed quality status are excluded.
